Maxik

Maxik is a personal to-do assistant app for Google Tasks.

Purpose of the Maxik app

Maxik is a personal assistant application. Its purpose is to help its owner manage a to-do list kept in Google Tasks by sending short text or voice requests to the assistant in a private chat.

This page is public. No account, sign-up or login is needed to read it.

What Maxik does with Google Tasks

  • Shows the owner's task lists and the tasks in them.
  • Adds a new task when the owner asks for it, with a title, notes and a due date.
  • Marks a task as completed when the owner asks for it.
  • Deletes a task when the owner asks for it.

Google data Maxik uses

Maxik requests one Google permission: access to Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks). It uses this permission only for the actions listed above. Maxik does not access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Photos, contacts or any other Google data.

What Maxik does not do

  • Maxik does not generate, edit or process images or videos of people.
  • Maxik does not sell data, show advertising or share Google data with other people.
  • Maxik does not use Google data to train AI models.

Who uses Maxik

Maxik is a private app used by one person, its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
